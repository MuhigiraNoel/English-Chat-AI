🌍 About SpeakUp Global

SpeakUp Global is an online community where people from all over the world can practice speaking English through real-time voice conversations. Our goal is to help learners improve their confidence, pronunciation, fluency, and communication skills by talking with real people in friendly voice chat rooms.

🎯 Our Mission

To make English speaking practice free, easy, and accessible for everyone, regardless of their country or background.

🚀 Our Vision

To make SpeakUp Global one of the world's leading voice chat platforms, connecting millions of learners from every country to practice English, build friendships, and communicate with confidence.

✨ Features

  • 🎤 Real-Time Voice Chat
  • 💬 Live Text Chat
  • 🌍 Multiple Language Rooms
  • 👤 User Profiles
  • 👑 Room Owner Controls
  • 🔒 Safe Community
